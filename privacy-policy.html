Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the BillDash Android app ("BillDash", "the app") and the website getbilldash.in, developed by Ncaps Infotech ("we", "us"), handle information.

Information you enter in the app

Business details, products, prices, bills, customers, expenses and sales records that you enter in BillDash are stored on your device. We do not receive or store this business data on our servers.

Device permissions

BillDash may request the following permissions, only to provide the related feature:

  • Bluetooth – to find and connect to a compatible Bluetooth thermal printer for printing bills.
  • Camera – to take photos for products or receipts, if you choose to use this feature.

You can deny or revoke these permissions at any time in your Android settings. The rest of the app continues to work.

Advertising

BillDash is free to use and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and app usage data to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Website

The getbilldash.in website does not use forms or accounts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and hosting services described above, we do not share your information with third parties.

Children

BillDash is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Because your business data is stored on your device, you can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling BillDash.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
