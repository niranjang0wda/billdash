Questions, answered

Is BillDash really free POS billing software?

Yes. BillDash is 100% free to download and use, with no limit on bills or products. An optional subscription removes ads.

Which receipt printers does BillDash support?

Most Bluetooth thermal receipt printers in 2, 3 and 4 inch (58, 80 and 100 mm) sizes. You can also print through any printer set up on Android, or share the bill as a PDF.

Does BillDash support GST billing?

Yes. Set GST as included in the price or added on top for each item, choose the 5%, 12%, 18% or 28% slab, and add your GSTIN to receipts.

Can I use BillDash on a tablet?

Yes. On tablets BillDash shows the menu and the current bill side by side, which is ideal for a billing counter.

Is my billing data safe?

Your bills are stored on your own device. Turn on auto backup and restore everything on a new phone in one tap.
